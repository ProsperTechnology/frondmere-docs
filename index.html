Grow with Frondmere

Development documentation for Frondmere, a Shopify theme for independent plant nurseries. The theme is not yet approved for sale.

This is a Shopify theme for merchants. The illustrative nursery is a theme demonstration, not a retail business. This development build is not Theme Store approved and is not submission ready.

Install And Customize

Upload the theme-only ZIP under Online Store > Themes > Import theme. Preview before publishing. Choose headings/body fonts and favicon in Visual identity. Configure color schemes in theme settings, then select a scheme in each section. Set the header logo and main menu; nested menus support three levels. Configure footer links and your own policies. Shopify supplies newsletter/contact submission processing; a theme does not provide an email marketing service.

Home Page

Botanical Hero: heading, introductory copy, image, caption and collection link.
Plant Finder: plant collection and native filter values. Requires the Shopify filters described below. Never publish preferences that your catalog cannot filter accurately.
Featured Plants: selected collection and product count.
Nursery Journal: photograph, original nursery story, link to a care article or about page.
Nursery Notice: add blocks for pickup, hours, shipping regions or seasonal policies. These are information blocks, not fulfillment rules. Enforcement belongs in Shopify shipping/pickup configuration or an appropriate service.
Replace all onboarding copy and placeholders with authentic licensed content before using a demo for submission. Demo images are separate assets and do not transfer as merchant product images on theme installation.

Plant Data

Create product metafield definitions under Settings > Custom data > Products. Namespace is frondmere. Use the exact keys below. Enable storefront access where available. Values belong to the merchant and must be checked against the actual species/cultivar and growing conditions.

Product fields in the frondmere namespace
KeyUse
botanical_nameSingle line text: verified botanical identity.
lightSingle line text: consistent controlled values, e.g. Bright indirect light.
difficultySingle line text: merchant-assessed care category, e.g. Easy.
sizeSingle line text: current sale size category, e.g. Small.
wateringMulti-line text: condition-based guidance, not an invented fixed schedule.
humidityMulti-line text: plant-specific humidity guidance.
mature_sizeMulti-line text: eventual size, with units and growing context.
pet_safetySingle line text: explicitly reviewed label with species scope.
safety_sourceMulti-line text: source title/URL, exact species/cultivar and reviewer.
potsList of product references: merchant-curated compatible pots.
pot_guidanceMulti-line text: opening diameter, nursery-pot clearance, drainage and variant limits.

Blank care values are omitted. Do not fill an unknown value with a reassuring default. An unset pet-safety value is not evidence that a plant is non-toxic. Avoid broad safe-for-pets promises: identify the animal species and distinguish toxicity information from ingestion suitability. Theme software cannot verify botanical identity or veterinary facts.

Finder And Filters

Configure storefront filters for light, difficulty, size and pet_safety in Shopify Search & Discovery. The theme uses native filter.p.m.frondmere.KEY parameters. Create the definitions first and use consistent product values. The finder reads available values directly from the configured native filters. When a chosen collection has no filter context on the homepage, it loads the same section through Shopify's collection-context Section Rendering API. A failed request preserves the ordinary browse link; no option values are invented. Missing filters are hidden; with none configured, the section offers a normal browse link rather than an inaccurate matching form. Translate values using supported Shopify mechanisms. Verify every preference returns the correct subset, including no-result and unknown-data cases. Shopify imposes collection/filter limits; check current limits before enabling a large catalog. A theme cannot bypass native filtering restrictions. Sorting and ordinary collection filters use native URLs and standard GET forms.

Products And Pots

Configure product options and real variant inventory in Shopify. Product options navigate through Shopify option-value URLs; the page refreshes and Shopify determines the selected variant. The code does not embed the full variant array. A native 299-variant fixture and an impossible combination were checked during development. Retest your own catalog, variants, prices and media before publishing.
Curate pots with opening measurements and variant-specific checks. Recommendations are separate products, not an inventory-linked bundle. This build does not automatically select a pot variant, guarantee compatibility, discount a bundle, or combine inventory.

Pickup And Shipping

Enable native pickup at the correct locations and assign inventory by location. Pickup text reflects Shopify availability. Seasonal shipping copy is guidance; it does not block checkout. Configure shipping profiles and checkout delivery rules in Shopify. Advanced weather holds, appointments and zone restrictions can require services/apps. Subscription plans require a selling-plan provider. Frondmere includes a built-in purchase-option selector. If you use a compatible subscription app widget, add the app block first, then enable "Use an app for purchase options" in Main product or Featured plant. This hides the built-in selector; leave the switch off when no working app widget is present. Test one-time and subscription additions and variant changes. App compatibility can differ between product pages and homepage sections. The free Shopify Subscriptions app was sampled on a product page; its widget did not render in our homepage test. Recurring billing, contracts and customer notifications are supplied by the provider and Shopify, not the theme. Payment buttons require Shopify payment configuration.

Editable Product Blocks And Swatches

Main product and Featured plant provide separate vendor, title, botanical name, price, care summary, variant picker, quantity, buy buttons, pickup and description blocks. Reorder, hide, remove or add the supported blocks in the theme editor; keep an understandable title, price, option selection and purchase flow for your catalog. App blocks and custom Liquid are separate supported block types. Removing all built-in blocks leaves them removed.
Color and image swatches use Shopify option-value swatch data, configured through supported category metafields and option connections. A color name alone does not create a guessed swatch. The text option label remains visible. Shopify determines available combinations, prices and stock. Review your own category and variant connections.

Social Links And Localization

Add your business social profile URLs in theme settings. Blank URLs are omitted. The six built-in icons have readable accessible names. Theme settings do not create social accounts.
Publish languages and markets through Shopify and supply translated content through supported tools. Frondmere renders the active locale and applies right-to-left direction to supported RTL language codes. The free Translate & Adapt app was used only in the isolated QA store; app installation is not bundled with the theme. Our French/Arabic samples used English fallback text and do not verify translation quality or currency conversion.
Cart quantity and note updates use Shopify's locale-aware Ajax endpoint when JavaScript is available and return to the selected-language cart. Standard form submission remains available without JavaScript, but Shopify may return the default-language cart after an update. Native Remove links preserve the selected-language return path. Cart and payment processing remain Shopify services.

Gift-Card Recipients

If your store has activated Shopify gift cards and accepted applicable terms, eligible gift-card products expose optional recipient details. The send-on date accepts today through 90 days ahead in the shopper's local calendar; recipient name and message limits are 255 and 200 characters. Accelerated purchase buttons are omitted for gift-card products so recipient details follow the regular product form. Delivery, issuance, redemption, wallet behavior and service availability are provided by Shopify and require merchant configuration. These service flows have not been verified in this development build; the editor sample and local form checks are limited evidence.

Accessibility And Performance

Use descriptive image alt text for informative images and readable link labels. Keep text contrast adequate after changing colors. Check your actual fonts, mobile navigation, filters, product forms and cart with keyboard and screen readers. Do not embed essential text/buttons in images. Upload appropriately sized images. Keep essential homepage sections populated during performance checks. A clean source check does not certify accessibility or performance.

Known Gaps

This is still a development build. Consult the publisher QA checklist for unresolved native integrations, physical-device/browser and screen-reader coverage, external video and AR, pickup, subscriptions, gift-card delivery/redemption, localization, and final listing/legal review. Local Lighthouse and fixture results are scoped evidence, not full certification. Actual nursery stock must be reviewed by the merchant.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an app for the plant finder?

The theme supplies the finder. Configure its native Shopify filters with Shopify’s free Search & Discovery app.

Why is a care card or pet-safety label missing?

Fill the relevant product fields. Pet-safety labels require both source information and a review date. Missing data is omitted.

Does the theme verify a plant’s safety?

No. Your nursery must verify the botanical identity and source information for the actual stock. Images and software are not safety evidence.

Are recommended pots a bundle?

No. They are separate products chosen by the merchant. Check actual variant measurements and configure any bundle inventory or discounts separately.

Are the demonstration photos included with products?

No. Upload your own licensed product media and choose your own section images.

What does theme support cover?

Prosper Technologies supports theme defects and questions about documented settings. Store operation, plant-care verification, custom design work and third-party app development are separate responsibilities. This development build has no guaranteed response-time commitment.
