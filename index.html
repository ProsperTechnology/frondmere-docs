Grow with Frondmere

Development documentation for Frondmere, a Shopify theme for independent plant nurseries. The theme is not yet approved for sale.

This is a Shopify theme for merchants. The illustrative nursery is a theme demonstration, not a retail business. This development build is not Theme Store approved and is not submission ready.

Install And Customize

Upload the theme-only ZIP under Online Store > Themes > Import theme. Preview before publishing. Choose headings/body fonts and favicon in Visual identity. Configure color schemes in theme settings, then select a scheme in each section. Set the header logo and main menu; nested menus support three levels. Configure footer links and your own policies. Shopify supplies newsletter/contact submission processing; a theme does not provide an email marketing service.

Home Page

Botanical Hero: heading, introductory copy, image, caption and collection link.
Plant Finder: plant collection and native filter values. Requires the Shopify filters described below. Never publish preferences that your catalog cannot filter accurately.
Featured Plants: selected collection and product count.
Nursery Journal: photograph, original nursery story, link to a care article or about page.
Nursery Notice: add blocks for pickup, hours, shipping regions or seasonal policies. These are information blocks, not fulfillment rules. Enforcement belongs in Shopify shipping/pickup configuration or an appropriate service.
Replace all onboarding copy and placeholders with authentic licensed content before using a demo for submission. Demo images are separate assets and do not transfer as merchant product images on theme installation.

Plant Data

Create product metafield definitions under Settings > Custom data > Products. Namespace is frondmere. Use the exact keys below. Enable storefront access where available. Values belong to the merchant and must be checked against the actual species/cultivar and growing conditions.

Product fields in the frondmere namespace
KeyUse
botanical_nameSingle line text: verified botanical identity.
lightSingle line text: consistent controlled values, e.g. Bright indirect light.
difficultySingle line text: merchant-assessed care category, e.g. Easy.
sizeSingle line text: current sale size category, e.g. Small.
wateringMulti-line text: condition-based guidance, not an invented fixed schedule.
humidityMulti-line text: plant-specific humidity guidance.
mature_sizeMulti-line text: eventual size, with units and growing context.
pet_safetySingle line text: explicitly reviewed label with species scope.
safety_sourceMulti-line text: source title/URL, exact species/cultivar and reviewer.
potsList of product references: merchant-curated compatible pots.
pot_guidanceMulti-line text: opening diameter, nursery-pot clearance, drainage and variant limits.

Blank care values are omitted. Do not fill an unknown value with a reassuring default. An unset pet-safety value is not evidence that a plant is non-toxic. Avoid broad safe-for-pets promises: identify the animal species and distinguish toxicity information from ingestion suitability. Theme software cannot verify botanical identity or veterinary facts.

Finder And Filters

Configure storefront filters for light, difficulty, size and pet_safety in Shopify Search & Discovery. The theme uses native filter.p.m.frondmere.KEY parameters. Create the definitions first and use consistent product values. The finder reads available values directly from the configured native filters. Missing filters are hidden; with none configured, the section offers a normal browse link rather than an inaccurate matching form. Translate values using supported Shopify mechanisms. Verify every preference returns the correct subset, including no-result and unknown-data cases. Shopify imposes collection/filter limits; check current limits before enabling a large catalog. A theme cannot bypass native filtering restrictions. Sorting and ordinary collection filters use native URLs and standard GET forms.

Products And Pots

Configure product options and real variant inventory in Shopify. Product options navigate through Shopify option-value URLs; the page refreshes and Shopify determines the selected variant. The code does not embed the full variant array. High-variant and impossible-combination coverage still requires testing.
Curate pots with opening measurements and variant-specific checks. Recommendations are separate products, not an inventory-linked bundle. This build does not automatically select a pot variant, guarantee compatibility, discount a bundle, or combine inventory.

Pickup And Shipping

Enable native pickup at the correct locations and assign inventory by location. Pickup text reflects Shopify availability. Seasonal shipping copy is guidance; it does not block checkout. Configure shipping profiles and checkout delivery rules in Shopify. Advanced weather holds, appointments and zone restrictions can require services/apps. Subscription plans require a selling-plan provider. Payment buttons require Shopify payment configuration.

Accessibility And Performance

Use descriptive image alt text for informative images and readable link labels. Keep text contrast adequate after changing colors. Check your actual fonts, mobile navigation, filters, product forms and cart with keyboard and screen readers. Do not embed essential text/buttons in images. Upload appropriately sized images. Keep essential homepage sections populated during performance checks. A clean source check does not certify accessibility or performance.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an app for the plant finder?

The theme supplies the finder. Configure its native Shopify filters with Shopify’s free Search & Discovery app.

Why is a care card or pet-safety label missing?

Fill the relevant product fields. Pet-safety labels require both source information and a review date. Missing data is omitted.

Does the theme verify a plant’s safety?

No. Your nursery must verify the botanical identity and source information for the actual stock. Images and software are not safety evidence.

Are recommended pots a bundle?

No. They are separate products chosen by the merchant. Check actual variant measurements and configure any bundle inventory or discounts separately.

Are the demonstration photos included with products?

No. Upload your own licensed product media and choose your own section images.

What does theme support cover?

Prosper Technologies supports theme defects and questions about documented settings. Store operation, plant-care verification, custom design work and third-party app development are separate responsibilities. This development build has no guaranteed response-time commitment.
